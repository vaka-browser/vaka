# Code of conduct

Vaka is built for families, kids and people who are not security experts. The community around it should feel the same way: welcoming, patient and honest.

## We expect

- Kindness. Assume good intent, especially with newcomers and non-native English or Swedish speakers.
- Constructive feedback. Criticise the code or the idea, never the person.
- Honesty about what you tested and what you did not.

## We do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or PRs, and block contributors who break these rules. If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately through the **Security** tab (Report a vulnerability) — it reaches the same people and stays confidential.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
